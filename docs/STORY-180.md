## 1. LOGLINE

## 6. AUDIO PLAN & SOUND DESIGN

Perancangan lapisan audio terkalibrasi (*4-Layer Soundstage*) untuk memberikan bobot visual tanpa menutupi kejernihan narasi vokal.

```
[Layer 1] VOICE OVER (id-ID-ArdiNeural / Human Pro)  : -16 LUFS (Focal Center)
[Layer 2] OCEAN AMBIENCE BED (Deburan Ombak & Angin) : -28 LUFS (Duck down to -36 LUFS during VO)
[Layer 3] PNEUMATIC HISS & ROTOR HUM (SFX Teknis)    : -24 LUFS (Kinetik sinkron gerak f(gf))
[Layer 4] SUB-BASS DRONE BED (Nada Rekayasa Rendah)  : -34 LUFS (Fondasi stabilitas)
```
